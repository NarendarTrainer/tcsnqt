# question
```
Given a number, find the sum of its digits using recursion.

Explanation:

. Sum the last digit and recurse on the rest.

Input Format:

. A single integer n.

Output Format:

· A single integer (sum of digits).

Constraints:

. 0≤n≤109

Sample Input:

12345

Sample Output:

15

Explanation of Output:

1+2+3+4+5=15
```
